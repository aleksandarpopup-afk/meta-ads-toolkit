Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026
Srpska verzija ↓

PerfMind (www.perfmind.tech) is a marketing analytics web application for advertising agencies and marketers. It connects to Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads and turns that data into reports, insights and answers. PerfMind is operated by Aleksandar Matijević, Serbia (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data we process, why, and what control you have over it.

1. Data we collect

  • Account identifier: a random identifier (UUID) created when you first open the app and stored in your browser. We do not currently collect your name, email address or password to use the app.
  • Data you enter: client names, budgets, spend entries, notification settings and files you upload (for example screenshots or CSV exports from advertising platforms).
  • Google user data (only after you connect it):
    • Google Analytics 4 – read-only access to reporting data of the properties you select (for example revenue, purchases, products, traffic sources, campaigns, devices and locations).
    • Google Ads – access to reporting data of the accounts you select (for example cost, clicks, impressions, conversions, campaigns, keywords and search terms). PerfMind only reads this data; it never creates, changes, pauses or deletes anything in your Google Ads accounts.
    • The OAuth tokens needed to keep these connections active.
  • Push notification data: if you enable notifications, the technical subscription data your browser provides for delivering them.

2. How we use the data

We use the data only to provide PerfMind's features to you: dashboards, campaign and product analysis, budget pacing, reports, notifications and answers to the questions you ask. To create written insights and answers, the relevant data is processed by an AI model provider (Anthropic) on our behalf, only to generate the response you requested.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, share it with data brokers, or use it to train or improve general AI models.

Google API Services – Limited Use. PerfMind's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide and improve user-facing features of PerfMind, is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide those features, for security purposes or to comply with law, and is never read by humans unless you give explicit permission, it is necessary for security or legal reasons, or the data is aggregated and anonymized.

3. Service providers

We rely on the following providers to run PerfMind. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Vercel – hosting of the application;
  • Supabase – database (data is encrypted at rest);
  • Anthropic – AI processing for insights, reports and answers;
  • Google – the data sources you connect (Google Analytics, Google Ads).

4. Storage and retention

Data is stored for as long as you use PerfMind. Data you delete in the app (clients, budgets, imports) is removed from our database. When you disconnect a Google account or delete a client, we stop accessing that data. You can ask us at any time to delete all data related to your account, and we will do so within 30 days.

5. Your control

  • Disconnect Google Analytics or Google Ads at any time in the app (Clients → Disconnect).
  • Revoke PerfMind's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete clients, budgets and imports directly in the app.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by email.

6. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Access tokens and data are stored on secured infrastructure with restricted access. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

7. Browser extension (discontinued)

An earlier Chrome extension of this product, used to capture a screenshot of the active tab on the user's click, has been discontinued. It did not collect any data in the background.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the app.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: support@perfmind.tech

Politika privatnosti

Poslednje ažurirano: 8. novembar 2026.

PerfMind (www.perfmind.tech) je web aplikacija za analitiku marketinga, namenjena agencijama i marketarima. Povezuje se sa Google Analytics 4 i Google Ads-om i pretvara te podatke u izveštaje, uvide i odgovore. PerfMind vodi Aleksandar Matijević, Srbija („mi“). Ova politika objašnjava koje podatke obrađujemo, zašto, i kakvu kontrolu imate nad njima.

1. Podaci koje prikupljamo

  • Identifikator naloga: nasumični identifikator (UUID) koji se pravi pri prvom otvaranju aplikacije i čuva u vašem pregledaču. Trenutno ne prikupljamo ime, mejl adresu ni lozinku.
  • Podaci koje unosite: nazivi klijenata, budžeti, unosi potrošnje, podešavanja obaveštenja i fajlovi koje okačite (npr. snimci ekrana ili CSV izvozi iz oglasnih platformi).
  • Google podaci (tek kada ih povežete):
    • Google Analytics 4 – pristup samo za čitanje izveštajnim podacima izabranih property-ja (npr. prihod, kupovine, proizvodi, izvori saobraćaja, kampanje, uređaji i lokacije).
    • Google Ads – pristup izveštajnim podacima izabranih naloga (npr. trošak, klikovi, prikazi, konverzije, kampanje, ključne reči i pojmovi pretrage). PerfMind ove podatke samo čita i nikada ništa ne pravi, menja, pauzira niti briše u vašim Google Ads nalozima.
    • OAuth tokeni potrebni da bi ove veze ostale aktivne.
  • Podaci za obaveštenja: ako uključite obaveštenja, tehnički podaci o pretplati koje vaš pregledač daje radi njihove dostave.

2. Kako koristimo podatke

Podatke koristimo isključivo da bismo vam pružili funkcije PerfMind-a: preglede, analizu kampanja i proizvoda, praćenje budžeta, izveštaje, obaveštenja i odgovore na vaša pitanja. Za pisane uvide i odgovore, relevantni podaci se obrađuju kod pružaoca AI modela (Anthropic), u naše ime i samo radi odgovora koji ste zatražili.

Vaše podatke ne prodajemo, ne koristimo za oglašavanje, ne delimo sa trgovcima podacima i ne koristimo za treniranje ili unapređenje opštih AI modela.

Google API servisi – ograničena upotreba. Korišćenje i prenos informacija dobijenih preko Google API-ja od strane PerfMind-a pridržava se Google API Services User Data Policy, uključujući zahteve o ograničenoj upotrebi (Limited Use).

3. Pružaoci usluga

  • Vercel – hosting aplikacije;
  • Supabase – baza podataka (podaci su šifrovani pri čuvanju);
  • Anthropic – AI obrada za uvide, izveštaje i odgovore;
  • Google – izvori podataka koje povežete (Google Analytics, Google Ads).

4. Čuvanje podataka

Podaci se čuvaju dok koristite PerfMind. Ono što obrišete u aplikaciji (klijenti, budžeti, uvozi) briše se iz naše baze. Kada otkačite Google nalog ili obrišete klijenta, prestajemo da pristupamo tim podacima. U svakom trenutku možete zatražiti brisanje svih podataka vezanih za vaš nalog, i uradićemo to u roku od 30 dana.

5. Vaša kontrola

  • Otkačite Google Analytics ili Google Ads u bilo kom trenutku u aplikaciji (Klijenti → Otkači).
  • Opozovite pristup PerfMind-a vašem Google nalogu na myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Brišite klijente, budžete i uvoze direktno u aplikaciji.
  • Zatražite pristup, ispravku ili brisanje podataka mejlom.

6. Bezbednost

Sve veze koriste HTTPS. Tokeni i podaci čuvaju se na zaštićenoj infrastrukturi sa ograničenim pristupom.

7. Ekstenzija za pregledač (ukinuta)

Ranija Chrome ekstenzija ovog proizvoda, koja je na klik korisnika pravila snimak aktivnog taba, je ukinuta. Nije prikupljala podatke u pozadini.
